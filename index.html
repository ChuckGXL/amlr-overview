    UPCOMING

    31 Dec 2026 · in daysDelivery target for 2026

    First standards on CDD, thresholds and group-wide requirements go to the Commission; several guidelines are published.

    1 Jan 2027 · in daysData collection for the 2027 selection

    Provisionally eligible institutions report through their national supervisor, January to March.

    1 Jul 2027 · in daysFirst selection for direct supervision begins

    Up to 40 cross-border institutions chosen; outcome communicated by the end of 2027.

    10 Jul 2027 · in daysThe regulation applies

    Obligations apply directly; Member States' transposition deadline for the directive.

    10 Jul 2029 · in daysFootball clubs and agents

    Later application date for the newly added sectors.
